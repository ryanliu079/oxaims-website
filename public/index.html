Questions about joining

Who can take part?

Oxford students from any subject and at any experience level. You don’t need a startup idea or previous coding experience.

Does my project need to become a business?

No. You can make something useful, try a creative idea or work on a research question. It doesn’t have to become a business.

Is there a joining fee?

No. OxAIMS is free to join.

How do I join?

Fill in our short sign-up form. We’ll email you when we have dates and membership details to share.

Got something you’d like to make?

Leave your email. We’ll let you know when you can join us.
