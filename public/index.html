Questions about joining

Who can take part?

Oxford students from any subject, at any level. No coding experience needed.

Does my project need to become a business?

No. Make something useful, creative or just curious.

Is there a joining fee?

No. OxAIMS is free to join.

How do I join?

Fill in our short sign-up form. We’ll email you with dates.

Got something you’d like to make?

Leave your email. We’ll let you know when you can join us.
