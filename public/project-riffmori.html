Member project

Riffmori

Mac app · Member project · In development

Contributors: Ryan Liu

Riffmori brings a little music performance to your Mac. Connect to Spotify, choose an animated performer to keep you company on your desktop, and follow along with lyrics and live translation as you listen.

For a more immersive experience, bring performers together on a virtual stage and create your own band.

Built as a native macOS app with Swift and a web-based interface, Riffmori explores how animation, music and translation can make everyday listening more playful.

Translation uses supported languages on macOS 15 or later. Screens show sample lyrics and simulated playback.
