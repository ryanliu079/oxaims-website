The workshop series

What would the workshops involve?

The plan is to help you go from trying the tools to making a small project of your own.

  1. 01 / Learn

    Learn the basics

    Across the six planned sessions, we’ll cover AI tools, GitHub and ways to test what you’ve made.

  2. 02 / Build

    Try your own project

    Choose a small idea, make a first version and try it out. Ask other students for help and feedback along the way.

  3. 03 / Show

    Show it to the group

    At the end, everyone will show their project, talk about what worked and explain what they’d change.

The year-end showcase is separate from the workshops. Teams will apply with a short pitch, and selected projects will present. We’re still working out the dates and format.
